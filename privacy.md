# Privacy

Batch Watermark Video is developed by [3thousand30](https://3thousand30.com). Watermarking happens locally through the FFmpeg engine bundled with the app.

3thousand30 does not collect, store, or receive your videos, filenames, watermark text, logo files, settings, or outputs. The app stores ordinary local interface preferences only, such as whether the first-run guide has been dismissed.

FFmpeg licence information is included with the installed app.
